The AI creation story
From concept to GDD
Authored a full Game Design Document (V1.0) using Google AI Studio — defining game mechanics, level systems, art style, colour palette, animation specs, and a complete video ad storyboard. Zero code, full product vision.
From GDD to shipped codebase
Evolved the GDD into a live production codebase (v1.0.7) — React + TypeScript + Vite frontend, Konva canvas rendering, local leaderboard, Google AdMob monetisation, and a Capacitor Android build pipeline. Submitted to Google Play Store. All designed through AI-powered workflow.
QA instincts applied to game design
7 years of production QA shaped every design decision — spawn fairness logic (inner 80% constraint), accessibility minimums (36px tap targets), edge-case handling (overlap checks, lane spacing), and a built-in debug suite with god mode, cheat codes, and live design mode.

Features shipped
🏆
Local leaderboard
Top 10 per difficulty stored locally. Personal best tracking. Best combo display. No login required — works fully offline.
🎯
Time-based physics
Frame-rate independent movement — consistent speed across all devices. Gravity 0.22, friction 0.985, max velocity 15.
✨
Juicy VFX
Tropical AoE blast with orange rings + particles. Thunder lightning bolts. Frosty freeze overlay. Confetti on combos.
💰
Real AdMob ads
Google AdMob SDK integrated. Interstitial every 3 games. Rewarded ad revive once per session. Live earning from day 1.
🛠️
Debug suite
God mode, score injection, bottle spawner, design mode with drag/scale/rotate — gated behind backtick key.
📖
Onboarding
4-step interactive tutorial overlay. Perfect pop demonstration. Shown once per device.
